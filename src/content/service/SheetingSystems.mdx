## Better than off-the-shelf

Because each system is made to measure, it fits your container properly. That gives a higher level of protection than off-the-shelf covers, keeping out the weather and keeping spills in.

- **Security built in** — we can add locking mechanisms and tamper-proof features to deter theft and vandalism.
- **Made for the weather** — durable materials that stand up to harsh conditions, season after season.
