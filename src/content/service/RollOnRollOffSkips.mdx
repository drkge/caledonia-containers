Roll-on roll-off (RoRo) containers are the workhorses of bulky waste — construction debris, demolition rubble, manufacturing scrap. When they're working hard every day, they need looking after properly.

## Refurbished to a like-new standard

We specialise in refurbishing RoRo containers. Our team cleans, repairs and upgrades each one, bringing it back to a like-new standard that meets the highest expectations for safety and performance.

## Better than before

Refurbishment isn't only cosmetic. We replace worn-out components, reinforce the structure and apply high-quality paint finishes, so each container works smoothly and reliably for years to come.

## Trailers built to match

To go with your RoRo containers, we custom-build trailers to transport them — from robust trailers for tough terrain to compact, manoeuvrable ones for urban work. Tell us how you operate and we'll build to suit.
