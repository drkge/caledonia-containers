<h2>
  Built to last, our sturdy, durable skips meet all your waste removal needs.
</h2>

<p>
  We build new skips with sturdy construction, fully welded and sealed floor,
  fixed roof section, reinforcement, cast Meiller tipping bar, full depth
  pressed channel fork truck sleeves, front protection, and Double Skin Duraflex
  polyurethane lids.
</p>

<details>
 <summary class="text-lg font-semibold">8 Yard Front End Loader Specification</summary>
  <p><strong>Front, Rear, and Side Walls:</strong> 3 mm Steel Plate Fully Welded on Exterior and Stitch Welded Interior.</p>
<p><strong>Floor Fully Welded:</strong> 3 mm Steel Plate Fully Welded Internal and External with Four Drain Holes if Required. (Please Specify)</p>

<p>
  <strong>Top Railings:</strong> 3 mm Pressed Steel Channel Fully Welded and
  Sealed with Fixed Roof Section Fully Welded with Deflector Plate Welded In If
  Required as Option (Please Specify)
</p>

<p>
  <strong>FEL Truck Pockets:</strong> 4 mm Pressed Channel Reinforced with Top
  and Bottom Gussets
</p>

<p>
  <strong>Side Rail/ Lifting Lugs:</strong> 76 mm Hot Rolled Steel Channel
  Incorporating Industry Standard Cast Meiller Skip Truck Lifting Lugs Welded
  Through the RSC Steel Channel Into Sidewall
</p>

<p>
  <strong>Skip Tipping Bar:</strong> 32 mm Mild Steel Tipping Bar with Angle
  Welded Plate Supports
</p>

<p>
  <strong>Fork Truck Sleeves:</strong> Pressed Channel Full Depth Fitted with 3
  mm Hot Rolled Angle Welded Into Front and Rear with Extra Gussets for Support
  and to Reduce Crushing When Setting Down
</p>

<p>
  <strong>Front Protection:</strong> Angle Plate Welded Into Corners to Reduce
  Collision Damage from FEL Truck Arms
</p>

<p><strong>Lids:</strong> Industry Standard Shanks Green Double Skin Duraflex Polyurethane Side Hinged Lids with Locking Mechanism for Doors Remaining Open and Locking Facility Lids</p>
</details>
