## Every container has value

With steel prices where they are, even badly damaged containers have resale value. Trading them in helps offset the cost of new containers — and a fleet of smart new bins does a lot for your company's image.

We'll even salvage the good steel from damaged containers to repair and refurbish others, making the most of every tonne and minimising waste.

## Refurbished, not just replaced

Any container can be expertly refurbished with shot blasting and painting, restoring it to its former glory. Give us a try — you'll be impressed by the transformation, both visually and financially.
